## Anti-Patterns Verdict

### LLM Assessment
OmniAnima has a distinct, commanding creative foundation: **The Electric Animation Blueprint**. However, the implementation is currently fractured across three conflicting eras:
1. `public/landing.html` successfully embodies the monolithic Bebas Neue + Electric Indigo (`#1f00ff`) + Patch Orange (`#ff622b`) aesthetic.
2. `public/index.html` relapses into 2023 crypto SaaS tropes with an interactive canvas particle background, radial gradients, and 80px blur orbs.
3. `public/app.html` drops `Bebas Neue` entirely on dashboard headings and modal headers, lapsing into generic sentence-case `Archivo`.
4. `public/css/style.css` contains over 77 residual `box-shadow` rules, 16px/18px bubbly border radii, and legacy purple hues (`rgba(91, 63, 209)`).
5. Application runtime toasts and empty states in `public/js/app.js` contain playful emojis (`🎨`, `🔍`, `🎉`, `⚠️`, `✨`, `🚀`, `🎬`) that undermine professional draughtsmanship.

### Deterministic Scan
Automated scan (`node detect.mjs --json public/landing.html public/app.html public/index.html`):
- **Findings count**: 19 findings across 3 targets.
- **Single font warnings**: `public/app.html:9` and `public/index.html:9` flagged for using only Archivo across active DOM elements, confirming the typographic disconnect where display headings fail to utilize Bebas Neue.
- **Radius deviations (9 findings)**: Micro-radii (2px, 3px) flagged in `landing.html`. Analysis indicates micro-elements (icons, check boxes, badges) legitimately require 2px–3px to prevent turning into pills, but layout cards (`.promise-art-box`, `.corner-marker-01`, `.diagonal-corner-box`) must be standardized to `5px`.
- **Color deviations (7 findings)**: Hardcoded Tailwind Slate colors (`#94a3b8`, `#64748b`, `#475569`, `#334155`) drifted into `landing.html` instead of system tokens (`#212121`, `#d3d3d3`, `#ffffff`).
- **Numbered markers (01..06) [False Positive]**: Flagged under generic AI scaffolding, but verified as an intentional architectural drafting element defined in `DESIGN.md`.

## Overall Impression
OmniAnima's blueprint concept is extraordinary and sets it apart from generic SaaS tools. However, the experience breaks down the moment a user transitions from the landing page to auth and the animation studio. Purging all 77 drop shadows, consolidating strictly to 2 fonts (`Bebas Neue` and `Archivo`), and eradicating emojis will make the app feel like a unified, precision drafting instrument.

## What's Working
1. **The Blueprint Visual Identity**: Monolithic Bebas Neue display headlines with sub-1.0 leading against `#1f00ff` electric indigo and pure white drafting surfaces.
2. **Sharp Vector Logomark**: Clean, unboxed SVG stylus nib and technical drafting frame completely replacing raster assets.
3. **Core Studio Capabilities**: Multi-layer canvas, zero-lag drawing engine, onion-skinning buffers, and universal video export.

## Priority Issues
- **[P0] Residual Drop Shadows & Blurs**: 77 occurrences of `box-shadow` and ambient blur orbs in `style.css` violate the strict zero-shadow mandate. Replace entirely with crisp 1px hairline borders (`#1f00ff`, `#d3d3d3`, `#ffffff`).
- **[P1] Font Inconsistency & Studio Typographic Disconnect**: 3 fonts are loaded (`JetBrains Mono` is redundant). Remove `JetBrains Mono` and apply `Bebas Neue` to all dashboard, section, and modal headings in `app.html` to eliminate the `single-font` warning and restore visual unity.
- **[P1] Legacy Purple Theme & Radius Bloat**: Stylesheet contains legacy `rgba(91, 63, 209)` focus rings and 16px/18px rounded cards. Enforce strict 5px architectural radius and electric indigo accents.
- **[P2] Emoji Clutter in Application Runtime**: Emojis in `public/js/app.js` (`🎨`, `🔍`, `🎉`, `⚠️`, `✨`, `🚀`, `🎬`) and unicode `✓` in `landing.html` pricing table. Replace with technical hairline SVG icons.
- **[P2] Destructive Frame Deletion Safety Rail**: Deleting a frame or wiping the canvas lacks an undo buffer or confirmation dialog.
